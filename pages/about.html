Keeping Khmer cooking the best.

Our mission is to make Khmer recipes easy to discover, cook and pass on to the next generation.

Khmer food culture

Regional traditions

From freshwater fish to fragrant rice, local ingredients give Khmer dishes their distinctive character. Each region brings its own flavors to the table.

Ingredients & techniques

Lemongrass, galangal and kaffir lime are pounded into kroeung. Steaming, simmering and grilling bring depth to everyday cooking.

Food & community

Recipes are shared around family tables and passed from one generation to the next. Cooking together keeps these traditions alive.
